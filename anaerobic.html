Anaerobic (অবাত)

Definition, anaerobic respiration, metabolism, organisms, examples and a knowledge-check quiz.

Definition · সংজ্ঞা

Anaerobic means not requiring oxygen, or occurring in the absence of free molecular oxygen (O₂). বাংলায়, অবাত বলতে অক্সিজেনের অনুপস্থিতিতে সংঘটিত বা অক্সিজেনের প্রয়োজন হয় না—এমন অবস্থা, জীব বা প্রক্রিয়াকে বোঝায়।

1. Introduction · পরিচিতি

Biological processes may occur with molecular oxygen or without it. Processes requiring oxygen are called aerobic; processes occurring without molecular oxygen are called anaerobic.

অক্সিজেনের উপস্থিতিতে সংঘটিত প্রক্রিয়াকে সবাত (Aerobic) এবং অক্সিজেনের অনুপস্থিতিতে সংঘটিত প্রক্রিয়াকে অবাত (Anaerobic) বলা হয়।

2. Etymology · শব্দের উৎপত্তি

The term is associated with Greek roots meaning “without”, “air” and “life”. French bacteriologist Louis Pasteur coined “anaérobie” in 1863.

  • an- — without / not (না বা বিহীন)
  • aer — air (বায়ু)
  • bios — life (জীবন)

3. Anaerobic Respiration · অবাত শ্বসন

Anaerobic respiration occurs without oxygen. Some organisms use other molecules as the final electron acceptor in their respiratory electron-transport chain.

অবাত শ্বসনে অক্সিজেনের পরিবর্তে কিছু জীব ইলেকট্রন পরিবহন শৃঙ্খলে বিকল্প ইলেকট্রন গ্রাহক ব্যবহার করে।

  • Nitrate (NO₃⁻) — নাইট্রেট
  • Fumarate — ফিউমারেট
  • Sulfate (SO₄²⁻) — সালফেট
  • Elemental sulfur (S) — মৌলিক সালফার

The supplied reference describes anaerobic respiration as generally less energy-efficient than aerobic respiration because the alternative electron acceptors have lower reduction potentials than oxygen.

4. Anaerobic Metabolism · অবাত বিপাক

Anaerobic metabolism produces energy without oxygen consumption. In substrate-level phosphorylation, a phosphate group is transferred from a phosphorylated intermediate to ADP, forming ATP.

During strenuous activity, muscles may rely more on anaerobic energy pathways when oxygen delivery cannot meet demand. The supplied article notes lactate formation and compares yields as 2 ATP per glucose for anaerobic metabolism and 36 ATP for aerobic metabolism; actual yields depend on pathway and context.

5. Anaerobic Organisms · অবাত জীব

Organisms that can live and carry out metabolism without oxygen are called anaerobes. Three groups:

5.1 Obligate anaerobes · আবশ্যিক অবাত

Require an oxygen-free environment; oxygen exposure can harm them. Examples in the supplied text: Bacteroides thetaiotaomicron, Peptostreptococcus, Prevotella.

5.2 Facultative anaerobes · ঐচ্ছিক অবাত

Can survive without oxygen but may use aerobic respiration when oxygen is available. Examples: Staphylococcus spp., Salmonella, Shewanella oneidensis.

5.3 Aerotolerant organisms · বায়ু-সহনশীল অবাত

Do not use oxygen for metabolism but tolerate its presence. Examples: Cutibacterium acnes, Lactobacillus rhamnosus, Streptococcus pyogenes.

6. Examples · উদাহরণ

  • Finegoldia magna — an anaerobic bacterial species.
  • Chytridiomycota — some representatives inhabit herbivore rumen environments and are associated with cellulose-rich plant digestion.
  • Loricifera — the supplied article describes members living in deep-sea environments with little or no oxygen.

7. Other Uses · অন্যান্য ব্যবহার

Anaerobic environment · অবাত পরিবেশ

An environment devoid of molecular oxygen.

Anaerobic infection · অবাত সংক্রমণ

Infections associated with anaerobic organisms. The reference lists some soft-tissue infections, brain abscesses, aspiration pneumonia and abdominal abscesses.

Anaerobic exercise · অবাত ব্যায়াম

Short, high-intensity activity relying heavily on energy pathways that do not require oxygen directly. Examples include sprinting and weightlifting.

Anaerobic threshold · অবাত সীমা

An exercise-physiology term for an intensity at which lactate begins to accumulate more rapidly in blood; the exact definition depends on measurement method.

8. Quick Summary · সংক্ষিপ্তসার

  • Anaerobic means without molecular oxygen — অবাত।
  • Anaerobes can live or metabolize without oxygen.
  • Three groups: obligate, facultative and aerotolerant anaerobes.
  • Anaerobic respiration may use final electron acceptors other than oxygen.
  • Sprinting and weightlifting are examples of anaerobic exercise.
